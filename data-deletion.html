Data Deletion Instructions

Oneday Clothing · Last updated: November 10, 2026

If you signed in to Oneday Clothing with Google, TikTok or Telegram and want your data deleted, follow the steps below.

Step 1: Send a deletion request

Email fennmybad@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data and tell us which sign-in method you used (Google, TikTok or Telegram) and the name, email or username on that account.

Step 2: We delete your data

Within 7 days we will delete your account and personal information (name, email, Google/TikTok/Telegram account ID and picture) and reply to confirm. Completed order records may be kept without any link to your identity, for accounting purposes.

Optional: revoke access from the sign-in provider

  • Google: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services → select Oneday → Remove access.
  • TikTok: TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services → select Oneday Cambodia → Remove.
  • Telegram: Telegram → Settings → Privacy and Security → Logged-in Websites (or Devices) → log out of Oneday.

See also our Privacy Policy.

数据删除说明

Oneday Clothing · 最后更新：2026年11月10日

如果您使用 Google、TikTok 或 Telegram 登录过 Oneday Clothing，并希望删除您的数据，请按以下步骤操作。

第 1 步：发送删除请求

发送邮件至 fennmybad@gmail.com，主题写 Delete my data，并说明您使用的登录方式（Google、TikTok 或 Telegram）以及该账户上的姓名、邮箱或用户名。

第 2 步：我们删除数据

我们将在 7 天内删除您的账户和个人信息（姓名、邮箱、Google/TikTok/Telegram 账户 ID 和头像），并回复确认。已完成的订单记录可能会在与您身份无关联的情况下保留，用于会计目的。

  • Google：Google 账户 → 安全性 → 第三方应用和服务 → 选择 Oneday → 移除访问权限。
  • TikTok：TikTok → 设置和隐私 → 安全与权限 → 应用和服务 → 选择 Oneday Cambodia → 移除。
  • Telegram：Telegram → 设置 → 隐私与安全 → 已登录的网站（或设备）→ 退出 Oneday。
