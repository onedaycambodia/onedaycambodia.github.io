Data Deletion Instructions

Oneday Clothing · Last updated: November 7, 2026

If you signed in to Oneday Clothing with Facebook and want your data deleted, follow the steps below.

Step 1: Send a deletion request

Email fennmybad@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data and include the name on your account (your Facebook name).

Step 2: We delete your data

Within 7 days we will delete your account and personal information (name, email, Facebook ID and picture) and reply to confirm. Completed order records may be kept without any link to your identity, for accounting purposes.

Optional: remove the app from Facebook

You can also revoke access yourself: Facebook → Settings → Security and login → Apps and websites → select Oneday Cambodia → Remove.

See also our Privacy Policy.
