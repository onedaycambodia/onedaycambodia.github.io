Privacy Policy

Oneday Clothing · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Oneday Clothing (https://onedaycambodia.github.io/) is an online clothing shop in Cambodia. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it when you sign in or place an order.

1. Information we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture. We never receive your Google password.
  • When you sign in with Telegram: your name, username, Telegram user ID and profile picture (and phone number, if you allow it).
  • When you sign in with TikTok: your TikTok display name, profile picture and a TikTok user ID (scope user.info.basic). We never receive your TikTok password and we do not post to or read your TikTok videos.
  • Information you enter when ordering, such as your name, phone number, delivery location and the items you order.

2. How we use it

  • To let you sign in and see your order history.
  • To process, deliver and confirm your orders.
  • To contact you about your orders.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is stored on our servers and may pass through technical services we use to run the shop (such as hosting and Telegram, which notifies the shop owner of orders).

4. Retention

We keep your account information while your account is active and delete it on request.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. See how to delete your data on the data deletion page.

6. Contact

Questions? Contact us at fennmybad@gmail.com
